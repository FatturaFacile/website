Privacy Policy & Regulatory Compliance

EU GDPR (Regulation 2016/679) & Swiss nLPA / FADP

Core Engineering Principle: At Fattura Facile, privacy isn't a legal afterthought; it's the architectural foundation of our software.

1. Standalone Desktop Version (Local SQLite)

When utilizing the USB portable Desktop version:

  • Zero Data Transmission: Billing records, customer databases, tax IDs, and bank account numbers are stored exclusively inside the local SQLite file on your device.
  • No Intermediary Servers: We do not collect telemetry, track usage, or harvest invoice information.
  • Privacy Governance: The business user acts as the sole Data Controller. No cross-border data transfer occurs and no Data Processing Agreement (DPA) with us is necessary for local usage.

2. Self-Hosted Cloud Version (Hosting managed by client)

Even when running the networked edition:

  • Client's Own Infrastructure: The system is deployed on your private servers or VPS. We have zero access to your database.
  • Direct Compliance: All GDPR and nLPA compliance aspects remain entirely within your internal control, avoiding third-party data breaches.
  • Direct contact: For questions or module requests: info@fatturafacile.eu.

3. Data Subject Rights

Under Articles 15-22 of EU GDPR and Articles 25-29 of the Swiss nLPA, individuals retain full rights of access, rectification, portability, and deletion. In the Desktop edition, you already possess full direct ownership of your raw database.
